# How to Create a Facebook Business Account

Since you don't have a Facebook account yet, follow these steps to get everything ready for your CRM integration.

## Phase 1: Create a Personal Facebook Account
*Note: Meta requires a personal profile to manage Business Pages.*

1.  Go to **[facebook.com](https://www.facebook.com)**.
2.  Click **"Create new account"**.
3.  Fill in your details (use your real name, as dummy accounts often get banned).
4.  Verify your email/phone number.

## Phase 2: Create a Business Page
*This is the public "Face" of your business on Facebook.*

1.  Log in to your new Facebook account.
2.  Go to **[facebook.com/pages/create](https://www.facebook.com/pages/create)**.
3.  **Page Name**: Enter your business name (e.g., "Solar Solutions Ltd").
4.  **Category**: Choose a relevant category (e.g., "Solar Energy Company").
5.  Click **Create Page**.

## Phase 3: Create a Lead Form (Your "Contact User" Form)
*This is the form customers will fill out.*

1.  Go to **[Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com)**.
2.  Navigate to **All Tools** -> **Instant Forms**.
3.  Click **Create Form**.
4.  **Form Type**: Select "More Volume".
5.  **Intro**: Add a headline (e.g., "Get a Free Solar Quote").
6.  **Questions**:
    *   Ensure **Full Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** are included.
    *   *These fields map directly to your CRM.*
7.  **Privacy Policy**: Add a link to your website's privacy policy (or a generic one if testing).
8.  **Publish** the form.

---

### 🎉 You are now ready!
Once these steps are done, return to `FACEBOOK_INTEGRATION.md` to connect the CRM using your new accounts.
